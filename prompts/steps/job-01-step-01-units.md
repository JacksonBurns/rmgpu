## Goal

Port the Quantity semantics of RMG's cimported quantity.py to a thin
pint-backed class. This class replaces RMG's Quantity everywhere - get the
semantics right now.

## Deliverables

- rmgpu/units.py: `Quantity` over pint:
    constructors from (value, unit) tuples AND from "1350 K" strings (the
    legacy importer needs both); `.to_si()`; arithmetic (+, -, *, / by scalar
    and by Quantity with unit checking - wrong-unit ops raise);
    equality with unit normalization; `.value_in(unit)`; repr.
    SI-internal (J, K, Pa, mol, kg) as the canonical storage.
- Unit tests in tests/test_units.py: construction both ways, arithmetic,
  unit-mismatch raises, SI conversion against hand-computed values,
  string-parse edge cases (negative values, exponents, "kmol/m^3"-style).
