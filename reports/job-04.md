**Finding (thermo): the H298 target is NOT Hf(298).** The model is bounded to
positive values (trained on 1662 rmgdb species, all H298 > 0 - PLAN 3b
flagged gap), AND even near-zero elements (H2, O2, Ar, N2, He, Cl2, F2;
|ref| < 5 kJ/mol) show mean +293 kJ/mol, tracking atomization energy - i.e.
the output behaves like absolute H(298), not enthalpy of formation. Negative
Hf298 species (12 of 46: CH4, NH3, O(S), CO, CO3s1, CO3t1, N2, HCl, HBr,
HF, OCCCO, ethane) can never be represented. S298/Cp are the comparatively
sound properties (S298 worst: OCCO(S) -74.4, C3H2 +62.1 J/mol/K; Cp p95 17.1).
